# Product Overview

## Sumit.Tech - Personal Portfolio Website

A modern, responsive personal portfolio website showcasing web development and design work. The site serves as a professional showcase for Sumit Hirawat's skills, projects, and contact information.

### Key Features
- Dark theme with sky blue brand colors (#38bdf8, #0ea5e9)
- Smooth scroll animations and loading screen
- Mobile-responsive design with glassmorphism effects
- Project showcase with filtering capabilities
- Skills visualization and contact integration
- Social media integration (GitHub, LinkedIn, Instagram)

### Target Audience
- Potential clients and employers
- Fellow developers and designers
- Professional network contacts

### Brand Identity
- Clean, modern aesthetic with dark theme
- Sky blue accent colors for highlights and CTAs
- Professional yet approachable tone
- Focus on technical expertise and design skills
